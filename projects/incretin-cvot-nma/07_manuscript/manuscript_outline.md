## 1. Project Information

- **Title**: Comparative Cardiovascular Efficacy of Incretin-Based Therapies in Patients with Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease: A Bayesian Network Meta-Analysis of Cardiovascular Outcomes Trials
- **Short Title**: Incretin Therapies and Cardiovascular Outcomes: A Network Meta-Analysis
- **Analysis Type**: Bayesian NMA (random-effects, P-score ranking)
- **PRISMA Extension**: PRISMA-NMA

## 2. Key Messages (3-5 take-home points)

1. All incretin-based therapies with completed CVOTs reduce 3-point MACE versus placebo, but the magnitude of benefit varies substantially across agents (HR range: 0.73-0.91)
2. Efpeglenatide and semaglutide SC show the highest P-scores for MACE reduction, but confidence intervals overlap substantially across agents
3. Semaglutide 2.4mg (SELECT) demonstrates significant MACE reduction in non-diabetic ASCVD patients, expanding the indication beyond T2DM
4. The network is dominated by placebo-controlled comparisons with one closed loop (tirzepatide-dulaglutide-placebo via SURPASS-CVOT), limiting precision of indirect head-to-head comparisons
5. Treatment ranking is consistent across sensitivity analyses (excluding non-T2DM trials, fixed-effects, leave-one-out)

## 3. Narrative Arc

- **Clinical problem**: Cardiovascular disease remains the leading cause of death in patients with T2DM and obesity; multiple incretin-based therapies now have CVOT evidence but no head-to-head comparisons exist for most pairs
- **Knowledge gap**: Existing NMAs are restricted to T2DM populations and miss recent trials (SELECT, SOUL, SURPASS-CVOT); no NMA includes non-diabetic ASCVD populations
- **Our contribution**: First comprehensive NMA including all incretin classes (GLP-1 RAs + dual GLP-1/GIP agonist) across diabetic and non-diabetic ASCVD, with SUCRA/P-score ranking
- **Clinical significance**: Informs evidence-based drug selection when multiple incretin options are available; identifies potential hierarchy for cardiovascular benefit
