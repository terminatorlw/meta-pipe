# Incretin-Based Therapies and Cardiovascular Outcomes: A Bayesian Network Meta-Analysis of CVOTs

## Research Question
In patients with established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD), how do different incretin-based therapies compare in reducing major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) versus placebo, and what is their relative ranking?

## PICO Framework

### Population
Patients with established ASCVD (prior myocardial infarction, stroke, or peripheral artery disease). Not limited to type 2 diabetes mellitus — includes trials enrolling non-diabetic patients (e.g., SELECT, SURMOUNT-MMO).

### Intervention
All incretin-based therapies with completed or ongoing CVOTs:
- **GLP-1 receptor agonists**: semaglutide injectable (SUSTAIN-6, SELECT), semaglutide oral (PIONEER 6, SOUL), liraglutide (LEADER), dulaglutide (REWIND), exenatide (EXSCEL), albiglutide (Harmony Outcomes), efpeglenatide (AMPLITUDE-O)
- **Dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonists**: tirzepatide (SURPASS-CVOT)
- **Emerging multi-agonists**: triple GLP-1/GIP/glucagon agonists (retatrutide, survodutide) — include if CVOT data available by April 2026

### Comparator
Placebo

### Outcomes
**Primary**: 3-point MACE (composite of CV death, non-fatal MI, non-fatal stroke)

**Secondary**:
- 4-point MACE (3p-MACE + hospitalization for unstable angina)
- Individual MACE components: CV death, non-fatal MI, non-fatal stroke
- All-cause mortality
- Heart failure hospitalization
- Coronary revascularization
- Stroke (fatal + non-fatal)
- Transient ischemic attack
- Peripheral arterial events
- Kidney composite outcomes (new macroalbuminuria, eGFR decline, renal death)
- Change in systolic blood pressure
- Change in body weight
- Change in HbA1c

## Analysis Type
**Bayesian network meta-analysis (NMA)** using random-effects model
- Indirect head-to-head comparisons between all individual agents
- SUCRA or P-score ranking
- League table with all pairwise comparisons
- Forest plots for each pairwise comparison
- Placebo as common comparator connecting the network

## Study Design
Randomized controlled trials (RCTs) only

## Databases
PubMed, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, Scopus

## Date Range
Inception to April 2026, English language only
