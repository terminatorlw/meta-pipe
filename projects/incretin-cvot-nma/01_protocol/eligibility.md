## Study Selection Criteria

### Inclusion Criteria

#### 1. Study Design

- Randomized controlled trials (RCTs)
- Cardiovascular outcomes trial (CVOT) design with adjudicated endpoints
- Minimum 1,000 patients enrolled
- Minimum 1 year median follow-up
- Phase III (phase II acceptable if CVOT-designed with adjudicated endpoints)

#### 2. Population

- Adults (>=18 years) with established ASCVD or high cardiovascular risk
- Established ASCVD defined as prior MI, stroke, or PAD
- With or without type 2 diabetes mellitus
- Non-diabetic populations eligible (e.g., obesity with ASCVD)

#### 3. Intervention

- Any incretin-based therapy:
  - GLP-1 receptor agonists: liraglutide, semaglutide (SC or oral), exenatide, albiglutide, dulaglutide, efpeglenatide
  - GLP-1/GIP dual agonists: tirzepatide
  - Triple agonists: retatrutide, survodutide (if CVOT data available)

#### 4. Comparator

- Placebo (primary)
- Active comparator acceptable if it connects the NMA network (e.g., SURPASS-CVOT: tirzepatide vs dulaglutide)

#### 5. Outcomes

Must report at least ONE of:
- 3-point MACE (CV death, non-fatal MI, non-fatal stroke) — required for primary analysis
- Individual MACE components
- All-cause mortality

---

### Exclusion Criteria

#### 1. Study Design

- Non-randomized studies
- Single-arm trials
- Observational studies (cohort, case-control)
- Phase I/II dose-finding studies without CV endpoint adjudication
- Reviews, meta-analyses, editorials, commentaries
- Study protocols without results
- Post-hoc analyses without primary trial data
- Trials with <1,000 patients or <1 year follow-up

#### 2. Population

- Type 1 diabetes mellitus exclusively
- Pediatric populations
- Populations without any ASCVD or CV risk enrichment

#### 3. Intervention

- Non-incretin therapies (SGLT2 inhibitors, DPP-4 inhibitors alone)
- Combination trials where incretin effect cannot be isolated
- Trials testing doses below approved therapeutic range

#### 4. Comparator

- No control group
- Active comparator that does not connect to the NMA network

#### 5. Outcomes

- No cardiovascular outcomes reported
- Only metabolic endpoints (HbA1c, weight) without CV events

## Detailed Eligibility Guidance

### Q1: Multiple Publications from Same Trial

**Scenario**: LEADER has original publication (2016), extended follow-up, and subgroup analyses.

**Decision**: Include the primary publication with longest follow-up. Extract data from supplementary publications for secondary outcomes. Avoid double-counting.

### Q2: Multiple Dose Arms

**Scenario**: SUSTAIN-6 has semaglutide 0.5 mg and 1.0 mg arms; AMPLITUDE-O has efpeglenatide 4 mg and 6 mg arms.

**Decision**: Combine dose arms for primary analysis (pooled vs placebo). Sensitivity analysis by dose if sufficient data.

### Q3: Non-Diabetic Population Trials

**Scenario**: SELECT enrolled overweight/obese patients with ASCVD but without diabetes.

**Decision**: INCLUDE. This expands the population but is central to the novelty of this NMA. Pre-specified subgroup analysis by diabetes status.

### Q4: Active Comparator Trial (SURPASS-CVOT)

**Scenario**: SURPASS-CVOT compared tirzepatide to dulaglutide (not placebo).

**Decision**: INCLUDE. This creates a closed loop in the NMA network (tirzepatide-dulaglutide-placebo), strengthening indirect comparisons. Treat dulaglutide arm as the comparator node.

### Q5: Trials with Mixed Primary/Secondary Prevention

**Scenario**: REWIND enrolled patients with established CV disease (~31%) and CV risk factors (~69%).

**Decision**: INCLUDE with the overall population. Extract ASCVD subgroup data if available for sensitivity analysis. Note proportion with established ASCVD.

### Q6: Conference Abstracts vs Full Publications

**Decision**: Prefer full peer-reviewed publications. Include conference abstracts only if no full publication exists AND sufficient quantitative data for extraction.

## Network Connectivity Requirements

For NMA validity, the included studies must form a connected network:

1. **Common comparator**: Placebo connects most nodes
2. **Closed loop**: SURPASS-CVOT (tirzepatide vs dulaglutide) + REWIND (dulaglutide vs placebo) creates one closed loop
3. **No isolated nodes**: Every treatment must connect to the network via at least one comparison
4. **Sufficient studies**: Minimum 1 study per comparison (ideally >=2)

## Screening Process

### Level 1: Title/Abstract Screening

**Include if**:
- RCT or CVOT of incretin-based therapy
- Reports cardiovascular outcomes
- Population includes ASCVD patients

**Exclude if**:
- Clearly not RCT
- Non-incretin therapy
- No cardiovascular outcomes
- Review/editorial/commentary

### Level 2: Full-Text Screening

Apply all eligibility criteria systematically. Document exclusion reasons.
