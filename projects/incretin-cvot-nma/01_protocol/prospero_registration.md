## 13. Review Question

In patients with established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD), how do different incretin-based therapies (GLP-1 receptor agonists, GLP-1/GIP dual agonists) compare in reducing 3-point major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) versus placebo and versus each other?

## 14. Searches

Electronic databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, Scopus. Trial registries: ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP. Manual search of reference lists and conference proceedings (AHA, ESC, ADA, EASD 2020-2026). Date range: inception to April 2026. Language: English only.

[To be uploaded as supplementary file]

## 16. Condition or Domain Being Studied

Atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD): coronary artery disease, cerebrovascular disease, peripheral artery disease. Major adverse cardiovascular events.

## 17. Participants/Population

Adults (>=18 years) with established ASCVD (prior myocardial infarction, stroke, or peripheral artery disease) enrolled in cardiovascular outcomes trials. Includes patients with and without type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Exclusion: Type 1 diabetes exclusively, pediatric populations, primary prevention populations without ASCVD.

## 18. Intervention(s), Exposure(s)

Incretin-based therapies with completed cardiovascular outcomes trials:
- GLP-1 receptor agonists: liraglutide, semaglutide (injectable and oral), exenatide, albiglutide, dulaglutide, efpeglenatide
- GLP-1/GIP dual receptor agonists: tirzepatide
- Emerging triple agonists (GLP-1/GIP/glucagon) if CVOT data available

## 19. Comparator(s)/Control

Placebo (primary comparator in most CVOTs). Active comparator (dulaglutide in SURPASS-CVOT) acceptable to connect the NMA network.

## 20. Types of Study to Be Included

Randomized controlled trials (RCTs) with cardiovascular outcomes trial (CVOT) design. Minimum 1,000 patients enrolled, minimum 1 year median follow-up, with adjudicated cardiovascular endpoints.

## 21. Main Outcome(s)

**Primary**: 3-point MACE (composite of cardiovascular death, non-fatal myocardial infarction, non-fatal stroke). Measured as hazard ratio (HR) with 95% credible interval from Bayesian NMA.

## 22. Additional Outcome(s)

- 4-point MACE (3p-MACE + hospitalization for unstable angina)
- Individual MACE components: CV death, non-fatal MI, non-fatal stroke
- All-cause mortality
- Heart failure hospitalization
- Coronary revascularization
- Stroke (fatal + non-fatal)
- Transient ischemic attack
- Peripheral arterial events
- Kidney composite outcome (new macroalbuminuria, eGFR decline, renal death)
- Change in systolic blood pressure (MD)
- Change in body weight (MD)
- Change in HbA1c (MD)

## 23. Data Extraction (Selection and Coding)

Dual independent screening at title/abstract and full-text stages. Disagreements resolved by consensus or third reviewer. Data extraction using standardized form including: trial name, drug, dose, sample size, population characteristics (age, % female, % T2DM, % prior MI), median follow-up, number of events and total patients per arm, HR with 95% CI for each outcome.

## 24. Risk of Bias (Quality) Assessment

Cochrane Risk of Bias 2 (RoB 2) tool for randomized trials. Dual independent assessment across 5 domains. GRADE/CINeMA framework for confidence in network meta-analysis estimates.

## 25. Strategy for Data Synthesis

Bayesian network meta-analysis using random-effects model. Software: R (gemtc, BUGSnet, or multinma package). Weakly informative priors for between-study heterogeneity. Treatment ranking via SUCRA. League table with all pairwise comparisons. Network geometry plot. Forest plots for each treatment vs placebo and head-to-head comparisons. Consistency assessment via node-splitting. Heterogeneity assessed via tau-squared and I-squared.

## 26. Analysis of Subgroups or Subsets

Pre-specified subgroups:
1. Diabetes status (T2DM vs non-diabetic)
2. Drug class (GLP-1 RA vs GLP-1/GIP dual agonist)
3. Administration route (injectable vs oral)
4. CV risk level (established ASCVD vs mixed primary/secondary prevention)

Network meta-regression: proportion with T2DM, mean baseline HbA1c, proportion with prior MI, mean follow-up duration.

## 27. Type and Method of Review

Systematic review with network meta-analysis
